Scanner limitations

This tool performs non-destructive, evidence-based checks only. It does not execute JavaScript, so reflected-XSS findings indicate potential execution that a human must confirm in a browser. SQL-injection findings are heuristic (error- and boolean-based) and never extract data. Absence of a finding is not proof of absence of a vulnerability.
